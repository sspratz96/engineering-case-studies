# Engineering Case Studies

This repository documents engineering case studies based on real-world problems I have worked on throughout my career.

Rather than focusing on source code, these case studies explain how technical decisions were made, why certain architectures were chosen, the trade-offs involved, and the lessons learned during implementation.

The goal is to share the engineering thinking behind building data platforms, automation systems, internal products and AI-enabled solutions.

---

## Topics

- Data Platforms and Data Architecture
- Data Governance
- ETL Pipelines
- Business Process Automation
- Machine Learning and Forecasting
- AI-enabled Systems
- FinOps
- Operational Analytics and Financial Reporting
- Product Engineering
- Local-First Applications
- Sensor Data Acquisition
- Computer Vision and Explainable AI

## Repository Philosophy

These case studies intentionally avoid publishing confidential business information or proprietary code.

Instead, they focus on engineering principles that can be applied across industries.
